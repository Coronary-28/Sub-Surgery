Arrange the five histological layers of the cornea in correct anterior-to-posterior order:
A) Epithelium → Stroma → Bowman's layer → Descemet's membrane → Endothelium
B) Epithelium → Bowman's layer → Stroma → Descemet's membrane → Endothelium
C) Endothelium → Descemet's membrane → Stroma → Bowman's layer → Epithelium
D) Epithelium → Descemet's membrane → Stroma → Bowman's layer → Endothelium
E) Bowman's layer → Epithelium → Stroma → Endothelium → Descemet's membrane
Correct Answer: B) Epithelium → Bowman's layer → Stroma → Descemet's membrane → Endothelium
Explanation: The correct anterior-to-posterior sequence is epithelium (regenerates fast) → Bowman's layer (does not regenerate) → stroma (~90% of thickness) → Descemet's membrane (basement membrane) → endothelium (pump layer, does not regenerate).

The main route of aqueous humour outflow, accounting for approximately 90–96% of drainage, is:
A) The uveoscleral pathway through the ciliary body face
B) Direct absorption by the lens
C) The trabecular meshwork and Schlemm's canal
D) The vitreous cavity
E) The central retinal vein
Correct Answer: C) The trabecular meshwork and Schlemm's canal
Explanation: Aqueous drains conventionally through the trabecular meshwork into Schlemm's canal and then into episcleral veins, accounting for the large majority of outflow; the uveoscleral route handles only about 4–10%. This conventional pathway is the main pharmacological target in glaucoma treatment.

Which structure is responsible for producing the outer lipid layer of the tear film, and what is its main function?
A) Lacrimal gland; provides 90% of tear thickness
B) Goblet cells; wets the hydrophobic corneal epithelium
C) Meibomian glands; retards evaporation of the aqueous layer
D) Accessory lacrimal glands; delivers antibacterial proteins
E) Conjunctival crypts; produces mucin
Correct Answer: C) Meibomian glands; retards evaporation of the aqueous layer
Explanation: The meibomian glands, located within the tarsal plates, secrete the outer lipid layer whose main job is to slow evaporation of the underlying aqueous layer. Dysfunction of these glands is a major cause of evaporative dry eye.

Contraction of which iris muscle produces mydriasis, and via which autonomic pathway?
A) Sphincter pupillae; parasympathetic (CN III)
B) Dilator pupillae; sympathetic
C) Sphincter pupillae; sympathetic
D) Ciliary muscle; parasympathetic
E) Dilator pupillae; parasympathetic (CN III)
Correct Answer: B) Dilator pupillae; sympathetic
Explanation: The dilator pupillae consists of radial fibres under sympathetic control, producing mydriasis, whereas the sphincter pupillae is circular and parasympathetically innervated (CN III), producing miosis.
###
Cones, responsible for colour vision and fine visual acuity, are exclusively found at which retinal location?
A) Optic disc
B) Ora serrata
C) Fovea centralis
D) Peripheral retina
E) Pars plana
Correct Answer: C) Fovea centralis
Explanation: Cones are concentrated at the macula and are exclusively present at its centre, the fovea centralis, which is the point of sharpest vision. Rods, by contrast, are far more numerous but absent from the very centre of the fovea, mediating dim-light and peripheral vision instead.
###
The optic disc corresponds to the physiological blind spot because:
A) It contains only rods
B) It contains no photoreceptors
C) It is the thinnest part of the retina
D) It lacks a blood supply
E) It is where the vitreous attaches most firmly
Correct Answer: B) It contains no photoreceptors
Explanation: The optic disc is where retinal ganglion cell axons converge and exit the eye as the optic nerve; since it contains no photoreceptors, it corresponds to the physiological blind spot. This is distinct from the fovea, which has the highest photoreceptor density.
###
The vitreous body is firmly attached at all of the following sites EXCEPT:
A) The vitreous base, just behind the ora serrata
B) The optic disc margin
C) The macula
D) The lens equator
E) None of the above — all are correct attachment sites
Correct Answer: D) The lens equator
Explanation: The vitreous body is attached to the vitreous base (peripheral retina/pars plana), the optic disc margin, and the macula. While its attachment to the macula and retinal vessels is relatively less firm than at the base or disc, it remains a critical attachment site. These locations are clinically significant because they experience traction and pulling when the vitreous shrinks with age. The lens equator is not a vitreous attachment site.
###
The conjunctiva contains goblet cells whose function is to:
A) Produce the outer lipid layer of the tear film
B) Secrete the mucin layer of the tear film
C) Produce aqueous humour
D) Regenerate the corneal epithelium
E) Provide sensory innervation to the ocular surface
Correct Answer: B) Secrete the mucin layer of the tear film
Explanation: Goblet cells within the conjunctiva secrete mucin, which allows the aqueous tear layer to wet the otherwise hydrophobic corneal epithelium evenly. The conjunctiva itself has bulbar and palpebral parts meeting at the fornices.
###
Which of the following is the correct pairing of a uveal tract structure with its principal role?
A) Iris — aqueous humour production
B) Choroid — supplies the outer retina via the choriocapillaris
C) Ciliary body — colour vision
D) Sclera — accommodation
E) Cornea — pupillary constriction
Correct Answer: B) Choroid — supplies the outer retina via the choriocapillaris
Explanation: The choroid is the posterior, vascular part of the uveal tract, and its capillary layer (the choriocapillaris) supplies the outer retina, including the photoreceptors. New vessel growth from the choroid through Bruch's membrane is also the underlying mechanism of wet age-related macular degeneration.

Select all correct statements regarding the three concentric layers of the globe:
A) The outer fibrous coat is composed of the cornea and sclera
B) The middle vascular coat (uveal tract) includes the iris, ciliary body, and choroid
C) The inner neural coat is the retina
D) The lens forms part of the outer fibrous coat
E) The globe wall is composed of these three concentric layers
Correct Answer: A) The outer fibrous coat is composed of the cornea and sclera / B) The middle vascular coat (uveal tract) includes the iris, ciliary body, and choroid / C) The inner neural coat is the retina / E) The globe wall is composed of these three concentric layers
Explanation: A, B, C, and E are all true — the globe wall consists of an outer fibrous coat (cornea + sclera), a middle vascular coat (uveal tract: iris + ciliary body + choroid), and an inner neural coat (retina). D is false because the lens is one of the three transparent refracting media inside the globe (with aqueous humour and vitreous), not part of the wall itself.
