The tear film:
A) Is 100 µm thick
B) Is composed of four layers
C) The mucin layer is in contact with the cornea
D) Is not important in the refraction of light entering the eye
E) None of the above
Correct Answer: C) The mucin layer is in contact with the cornea
Explanation: The tear film is trilaminar (lipid, aqueous, mucin), with the inner mucin layer directly wetting the hydrophobic corneal epithelium — making C correct and B wrong (three layers, not four). A is wrong because the tear film is only a few micrometres thick, not 100 µm. D is wrong because the tear-air interface is actually a major refractive surface of the eye, so the tear film is optically important, not irrelevant.

Aqueous humour is formed by:
A) Epithelium of ciliary body
B) Posterior surface of iris
C) Lens
D) Pars plana
E) Trabecular meshwork
Correct Answer: A) Epithelium of ciliary body
Explanation: B and C are wrong because the iris and lens play no role in aqueous production — the iris only regulates pupil size, and the lens is purely refractive. D is a plausible-sounding distractor since the pars plana is part of the ciliary body region, but production occurs at the ciliary processes (pars plicata), not the pars plana. E is wrong because the trabecular meshwork is a drainage structure, not a secretory one.

All of the following statements about the tear film are true EXCEPT:
A) The meibomian glands produce the outer lipid layer
B) The meibomian glands produce the aqueous layer of the tear film
C) The main and accessory lacrimal glands produce the aqueous layer
D) Conjunctival goblet cells produce the inner mucin layer
E) The lipid layer retards evaporation of the aqueous layer
Correct Answer: B) The meibomian glands produce the aqueous layer of the tear film
Explanation: The meibomian glands, located in the tarsal plates, secrete the outer lipid layer of the tear film, not the aqueous layer — the aqueous layer (about 90% of tear thickness) comes from the main and accessory lacrimal glands. A, C, D, and E are all correct descriptions of tear film anatomy, making B the false statement and the correct answer to this EXCEPT question.
###
Choose the correct answer about the retina:
A) Is 7 layers
B) Has ganglion cells whose axons form the optic nerve
C) Has three types of rods responsible for colour vision
D) The neuroretina is firmly attached to the RPE
E) The RPE delivers vitamin K for rhodopsin production
Correct Answer: B) Has ganglion cells whose axons form the optic nerve
Explanation: The retinal ganglion cell axons converge at the optic disc and exit the eye as the optic nerve — this is why the optic disc, lacking photoreceptors, corresponds to the physiological blind spot. A is wrong because the retina has 10 histological layers, not 7. C is wrong on two counts: colour vision is mediated by cones (not rods), and there are three types of cones (not rods). D is wrong because the neurosensory retina is only loosely apposed to the RPE (not firmly attached) — this is exactly why retinal detachment can occur. E is wrong because the RPE recycles vitamin A (not vitamin K) for the visual cycle and rhodopsin regeneration.
