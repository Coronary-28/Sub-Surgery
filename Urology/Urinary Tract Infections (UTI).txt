A female patient presents with flank pain and fever. She was treated with antibiotics, but after 3 days she still has fever and fatigue. What is the best next step in management?
A) Renal ultrasound
B) Add NSAIDs
C) CT scan of the abdomen and pelvis
D) Change antibiotics empirically
Correct Answer: C) CT scan of the abdomen and pelvis
Explanation:
Persistent fever despite 48–72 hours of appropriate antibiotics for pyelonephritis mandates a contrast CT to exclude a complication such as perinephric/renal abscess, obstruction, or emphysematous pyelonephritis; simply switching antibiotics or relying on ultrasound alone risks missing these findings.
