What is the most common presentation of renal cell carcinoma?
A) Gross hematuria
B) Flank pain
C) Incidental finding on imaging
D) Palpable flank mass
E) Classic triad of hematuria, pain, and mass
Correct Answer: C) Incidental finding on imaging
Explanation: Most RCCs are now discovered incidentally on imaging performed for unrelated reasons; the classic triad occurs in fewer than 10% of cases.
###
What is the most common histopathological subtype of renal cell carcinoma?
A) Clear cell RCC
B) Medullary RCC
C) Papillary RCC
D) Chromophobe RCC
Correct Answer: A) Clear cell RCC
Explanation: Clear cell RCC accounts for approximately 75% of RCC cases, is associated with VHL gene mutations, and carries the worst prognosis among the common subtypes.
###
The most common paraneoplastic syndrome associated with RCC is:
A) SIADH
B) Cushing's syndrome
C) Hypercalcemia
D) Polycythemia/erythrocytosis
Correct Answer: C) Hypercalcemia
Explanation: Hypercalcemia, mediated via PTHrP secretion, is the most common paraneoplastic manifestation of RCC.
###
A 64-year-old man presents with 2 months of painless visible hematuria, hypertension, weight loss, low-grade fever, and anemia. The most likely diagnosis is:
A) Lower urinary tract infection
B) Renal cell carcinoma
C) Urothelial bladder cancer
D) Prostate cancer
E) Adenocarcinoma of the bladder
Correct Answer: B) Renal cell carcinoma
Explanation: Painless visible hematuria with hypertension and constitutional symptoms (weight loss, fever, anemia) reflects the paraneoplastic and systemic presentation classically associated with renal cell carcinoma.
###
Renal cell carcinoma is associated with all of the following EXCEPT:
A) Vegetarian diet
B) Smoking
C) Obesity
D) Hypertension
Correct Answer: A) Vegetarian diet
Explanation: A vegetarian diet is not an established risk factor for RCC; recognized risk factors include smoking, obesity, hypertension, long-term dialysis (ESRD), and inherited syndromes such as VHL disease.
###
A patient presents with pneumothorax, cutaneous fibrofolliculomas, and a renal tumor. The most likely RCC subtype is:
A) Chromophobe RCC
B) Clear cell RCC
C) Papillary RCC
D) Medullary RCC
Correct Answer: A) Chromophobe RCC
Explanation: This triad is characteristic of Birt-Hogg-Dubé syndrome, which is associated with chromophobe/oncocytic renal tumors, skin fibrofolliculomas, and pulmonary cysts predisposing to pneumothorax.
###
A patient has an RCC measuring 4 cm confined to the kidney. The preferred treatment is:
A) Radical nephrectomy
B) Thermal ablation
C) Active surveillance
D) Partial nephrectomy (nephron-sparing surgery)
Correct Answer: D) Partial nephrectomy (nephron-sparing surgery)
Explanation: Partial nephrectomy is preferred for T1 tumors (≤7 cm) when technically feasible, as it provides equivalent oncologic outcomes to radical nephrectomy while preserving renal function.
###
Which of the following is NOT a recognized paraneoplastic manifestation of RCC?
A) Paraneoplastic erythrocytosis
B) Hypercalcemia
C) Increased serotonin level
D) Stauffer's syndrome
Correct Answer: C) Increased serotonin level
Explanation: Increased serotonin is not a recognized paraneoplastic feature of RCC; recognized paraneoplastic syndromes include erythrocytosis, hypercalcemia, hypertension, and Stauffer's syndrome (hepatic dysfunction without metastases).
###
A fat-containing renal mass identified on ultrasound is most likely:
A) Angiomyolipoma
B) Renal cell carcinoma
C) Oncocytoma
D) Transitional cell carcinoma
Correct Answer: A) Angiomyolipoma
Explanation: Macroscopic fat within a renal mass is pathognomonic for angiomyolipoma, a benign tumor composed of blood vessels, smooth muscle, and fat.
###
The classic triad of renal cell carcinoma consists of:
A) Fever, dysuria, and urgency
B) Frequency, nocturia, and weak stream
C) Edema, hypertension, and proteinuria
D) Hematuria, flank pain, and palpable mass
Correct Answer: D) Hematuria, flank pain, and palpable mass
Explanation: The classic (but now uncommon) triad of RCC is hematuria, flank pain, and a palpable flank mass, occurring together in less than 10% of patients.
###
The most common site of RCC metastasis is:
A) Bone
B) Liver
C) Lung
Correct Answer: C) Lung
Explanation: The lung is the most common site of metastasis for renal cell carcinoma, followed by bone and liver.
###
Which of the following is an established risk factor for renal cell carcinoma?
A) Alcohol consumption
B) Hypertension
C) Diabetes mellitus
Correct Answer: B) Hypertension
Explanation: Hypertension is a well-established risk factor for RCC, along with smoking, obesity, and long-term dialysis; alcohol and diabetes are not classically listed among the primary risk factors.
###
Most cases of renal cell carcinoma are discovered:
A) Incidentally
B) Due to the classic triad
C) Due to metastatic symptoms
Correct Answer: A) Incidentally
Explanation: The majority of RCCs are detected incidentally during imaging performed for unrelated indications, reflecting the often silent early course of the disease.
###
A 30-year-old pregnant woman presents with hypotension, flank pain, and Cullen and Grey Turner signs, with no prior similar episodes. The most likely diagnosis is:
A) Ureteric stone
B) Renal trauma
C) Ectopic pregnancy
D) Angiomyolipoma (spontaneous rupture)
Correct Answer: D) Angiomyolipoma (spontaneous rupture)
Explanation: Spontaneous retroperitoneal hemorrhage from a ruptured angiomyolipoma (Wunderlich syndrome) can present with flank pain, hypotension, and flank ecchymosis (Grey Turner sign), and is more likely to occur or become symptomatic during pregnancy.
###
A patient with RCC presents with elevated hemoglobin (erythrocytosis). Which hormone is responsible?
A) PTHrP
B) Erythropoietin
C) Renin
Correct Answer: B) Erythropoietin
Explanation: Ectopic erythropoietin secretion by the tumor causes paraneoplastic erythrocytosis in RCC; PTHrP causes hypercalcemia, not erythrocytosis.
###
A 42-year-old Black male with known sickle cell disease is diagnosed with RCC. The most likely subtype is:
A) Medullary RCC
B) Papillary RCC
C) Clear cell RCC
D) Chromophobe RCC
Correct Answer: A) Medullary RCC
Explanation: Renal medullary carcinoma is strongly associated with sickle cell trait/disease and typically occurs in young Black patients, distinguishing it from the other subtypes.
###
A patient is found to have a solid 4x5 cm renal mass in the lower pole. The next step in evaluation is:
A) CT without contrast
B) Ultrasound
C) CT with contrast
D) MRI
Correct Answer: C) CT with contrast
Explanation: Contrast-enhanced CT is the gold standard for characterizing a solid renal mass, as it allows assessment of enhancement (the key feature distinguishing RCC from benign lesions) and staging.
###
A key imaging characteristic of tumors associated with Von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) syndrome is:
A) Hypovascularity
B) Hypervascularity
C) Calcification
Correct Answer: B) Hypervascularity
Explanation: VHL-associated clear cell RCC tumors are characteristically hypervascular due to upregulation of angiogenic factors from loss of the VHL tumor suppressor gene.
###
Which of the following are established risk factors for renal cell carcinoma?
A) Alcohol, diabetes, and low BMI
B) ESRD, obesity, and hypertension
C) Hypertension only
D) Vegetarian diet and low salt intake
Correct Answer: B) ESRD, obesity, and hypertension
Explanation: ESRD, Smoking, obesity, and hypertension are all recognized, established risk factors for renal cell carcinoma.
###
Which of the following is a recognized paraneoplastic syndrome in RCC?
A) Anemia
B) Hypoglycemia
C) Hyperthyroidism
D) Polycythemia
Correct Answer: D) Polycythemia
Explanation: Polycythemia (erythrocytosis) due to ectopic erythropoietin secretion is a well-recognized paraneoplastic syndrome of RCC; RCC is in fact the most common cause of paraneoplastic erythrocytosis
