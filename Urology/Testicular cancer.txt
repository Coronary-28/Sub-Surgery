Most common histopathology in testicular tumors:
A) Seminoma
B) Embryonal carcinoma
C) Yolk sac tumor
D) Teratoma
Correct Answer: A) Seminoma
Explanation:
Seminoma is the most common single histological subtype of testicular germ cell tumors (~65%) and the most common testicular tumor in adults.
###
Cryptorchidism (undescended testis) is primarily associated with which complication?
A) Inguinal hernia
B) Infertility
C) Hydrocele
D) Testicular torsion
Correct Answer: B) Infertility
Explanation:
Cryptorchidism impairs spermatogenesis due to an unsuitable developmental environment and hyperthermia, and also increases testicular cancer risk.
###
Which of the following is an established risk factor for testicular cancer?
A) Smoking
B) Diabetes mellitus
C) Cryptorchidism
D) Hypertension
Correct Answer: C) Cryptorchidism
Explanation:
Cryptorchidism is one of the five well-established risk factors for testicular cancer, raising risk up to 40-fold; smoking, DM, and hypertension are not established risk factors.
###
Which of the following is NOT a risk factor for testicular cancer?
A) Family history of testicular cancer
B) Cryptorchidism
C) White race
D) Maternal preeclampsia
Correct Answer: D) Maternal preeclampsia
Explanation:
The established risk factors are white race, cryptorchidism, family history, personal history of testicular cancer, and germ cell neoplasia in situ; maternal preeclampsia is not among them.
###
Testicular tumors most commonly metastasize to which lymph node group?
A) Retroperitoneal lymph nodes
B) Inguinal lymph nodes
C) Iliac lymph nodes
D) Axillary lymph nodes
Correct Answer: A) Retroperitoneal lymph nodes
Explanation:
Testicular lymphatic drainage follows the gonadal vessels directly to the retroperitoneal/para-aortic nodes, not the inguinal nodes, unless the scrotal skin itself is involved.
###
What is the best initial management for a patient diagnosed with testicular cancer?
A) Trans-scrotal biopsy
B) Inguinal radical orchidectomy
C) Chemotherapy first
D) Radiotherapy only
Correct Answer: B) Inguinal radical orchidectomy
Explanation:
Inguinal radical orchidectomy is both diagnostic and therapeutic; trans-scrotal biopsy or orchidectomy is avoided because it risks seeding the scrotal lymphatics.
###
A 25-year-old man presents with a painless testicular mass and elevated β-hCG. What is the most appropriate next step?
A) Trans-scrotal biopsy
B) Abdominal CT then decide
C) Inguinal radical orchidectomy
D) Chemotherapy first
Correct Answer: C) Inguinal radical orchidectomy
Explanation:
Inguinal radical orchidectomy is the gold-standard initial management for suspected testicular cancer, providing tissue diagnosis while avoiding scrotal contamination.
###
What is the most common presentation of testicular cancer?
A) Weight loss
B) Hematuria
C) Fever
D) Painless testicular mass
Correct Answer: D) Painless testicular mass
Explanation:
The vast majority of testicular cancers present as a painless testicular mass, often found incidentally or by the patient.
###
A painless testicular mass should be considered which of the following until proven otherwise?
A) Testicular cancer until proven otherwise
B) Epididymal cyst
C) Hematocele
D) Hematoma
Correct Answer: A) Testicular cancer until proven otherwise
Explanation:
Any painless, firm intratesticular mass must be assumed malignant until excluded by ultrasound and, if needed, orchidectomy.
###
A 35-year-old man presents with a painless, hard scrotal mass he recently noticed. Which of the following is the most appropriate initial investigation?
A) CT scan of the abdomen and pelvis
B) Scrotal ultrasound
C) Trans-scrotal biopsy
D) Exploratory surgery
Correct Answer: B) Scrotal ultrasound
Explanation:
Scrotal (Doppler) ultrasound is the first-line investigation to characterize a testicular mass before any biopsy or staging imaging is pursued.
###
A 36-year-old man complains of painless testicular swelling and heaviness with a hard testis on examination, no fever, and a remote history of testicular trauma. Ultrasound suggests a mass. What is the most appropriate management?
A) Peritesticular hematoma — evacuation
B) Varicocele — varicocelectomy
C) Testicular mass — inguinal radical orchiectomy
D) Epididymo-orchitis — antibiotics
Correct Answer: C) Testicular mass — inguinal radical orchiectomy
Explanation:
A remote trauma history is a common distractor; a hard, painless mass confirmed on ultrasound is treated as testicular cancer with inguinal radical orchiectomy.
###
What is the most common complication if undescended testis (UDT) is left untreated?
A) Infection
B) Erectile dysfunction
C) Varicocele
D) Germ cell carcinoma
Correct Answer: D) Germ cell carcinoma
Explanation:
Untreated cryptorchidism markedly increases the risk of Germ cell (testicular) malignancy in addition to infertility.
###
A male patient presents with a testicular mass and elevated serum AFP and β-hCG. Which of the following tumor types is most consistent with this marker profile?
A) Embryonal carcinoma
B) Pure seminoma
C) Pure yolk sac tumor
D) Leydig cell tumor
Correct Answer: A) Embryonal carcinoma
Explanation:
Combined elevation of AFP and β-hCG indicates a nonseminomatous component; pure seminoma never elevates AFP, and "pure" yolk sac tumor typically elevates AFP alone, so embryonal carcinoma (or a mixed tumor) best fits both markers being raised.
###
Which of the following is NOT a common complication of undescended testis?
A) Infertility
B) Urinary tract infection (UTI)
C) Testicular malignancy
D) Testicular torsion
Correct Answer: B) Urinary tract infection (UTI)
Explanation:
The recognized complications of undescended testis are infertility, malignancy, and torsion; UTI is not a characteristic complication.
###
What is the recommended age for surgical orchidopexy in undescended testis?
A) At birth
B) Before 6 months
C) 6–12 months
D) After 5 years
Correct Answer: C) 6–12 months
Explanation:
If the testis remains undescended, definitive orchidopexy is ideally performed at 6–12 months of age.
###
Which type of testicular tumor characteristically elevates β-hCG only, without elevating AFP?
A) Yolk sac tumor
B) Teratoma
C) Embryonal carcinoma
D) Choriocarcinoma
Correct Answer: D) Choriocarcinoma
Explanation:
Choriocarcinoma secretes β-hCG and is notable for early hematogenous spread, but it never elevates AFP.
###
To which lymph node group does testicular cancer characteristically metastasize?
A) Para-aortic lymph nodes
B) Inguinal lymph nodes
C) Iliac lymph nodes
D) Mediastinal lymph nodes
Correct Answer: A) Para-aortic lymph nodes
Explanation:
Testicular lymphatic drainage follows the testicular vessels to the para-aortic (retroperitoneal) nodes, mirroring the embryologic origin of the testis.
###
What is the most common type of testicular cancer?
A) Teratoma
B) Seminoma
C) Choriocarcinoma
D) Yolk sac tumor
Correct Answer: B) Seminoma
Explanation:
Seminoma accounts for the largest proportion of testicular germ cell tumors and is the most common subtype in adults.
###
What is the most appropriate management for a 4-month-old male with a right-sided undescended testis?
A) Right-sided orchidopexy
B) Left-sided orchidopexy
C) Observation
D) Hormonal therapy
Correct Answer: C) Observation
Explanation:
At 4 months, spontaneous descent may still occur; definitive orchidopexy is reserved for testes still undescended at 6–12 months, so continued observation with follow-up is appropriate now.
###
In a patient presenting with a painless scrotal lump, which diagnosis must be excluded first?
A) Epididymal cyst
B) Hydrocele
C) Varicocele
D) Testicular cancer (germ-cell tumor)
Correct Answer: D) Testicular cancer (germ-cell tumor)
Explanation:
A painless intratesticular lump is considered malignant until proven otherwise.
###
What is the most appropriate investigation for a patient presenting with a painless scrotal lump?
A) Urgent scrotal ultrasound with Doppler
B) Trans-scrotal biopsy
C) CT abdomen/pelvis first
D) Serum PSA
Correct Answer: A) Urgent scrotal ultrasound with Doppler
Explanation:
Doppler ultrasound determines whether the lesion is intratesticular versus extratesticular and solid versus cystic; if suspicious, AFP, β-hCG, and LDH are sent, while trans-scrotal biopsy is avoided due to seeding risk.
###
A patient presents with a painless scrotal lump. Should the assessment proceed urgently rather than as routine follow-up?
A) Yes
B) No
Correct Answer: A) Yes
Explanation:
Given the risk of malignancy, a painless scrotal lump requires urgent urological assessment through a suspected-cancer pathway rather than routine follow-up.
