A young infertile male undergoes karyotype analysis. Which of the following is the most common genetic cause of male infertility?
A) 47,XYY
B) r(Y) (ring Y chromosome)
C) 45,X0
D) 46,XY/47,XXY mosaicism
E) 47,XXY
Correct Answer: E) 47,XXY
Explanation:
Klinefelter syndrome (47,XXY) is the most common sex chromosome abnormality and genetic cause of male infertility, presenting with small firm testes, azoospermia, and elevated FSH/LH with low testosterone.
###
Which of the following syndromes represents the most common chromosomal cause of male infertility?
A) Kallmann syndrome
B) Turner syndrome
C) Klinefelter syndrome
D) Noonan syndrome
Correct Answer: C) Klinefelter syndrome
Explanation:
Klinefelter syndrome (47,XXY) is the most common chromosomal abnormality causing male infertility by leading to primary testicular failure and azoospermia, whereas Kallmann syndrome is a genetic hormone disorder (hypogonadotropic hypogonadism) rather than a chromosomal aneuploidy, Turner syndrome exclusively affects females (45,X), and Noonan syndrome is an autosomal dominant genetic condition not characterized by a chromosomal number defect.
###
What is the first-line investigation of choice for a male presenting with infertility?
A) Seminal fluid analysis
B) Hormonal profile only
C) Scrotal ultrasound
D) Karyotyping
Correct Answer: A) Seminal fluid analysis
Explanation:
Semen analysis (seminal fluid analysis) is the first-line investigation in male infertility, assessing volume, sperm concentration, motility, and morphology; hormonal testing, ultrasound, and karyotyping are reserved for further work-up based on initial findings.
###
Infertility in males with cystic fibrosis is most commonly caused by which of the following?
A) Immotile sperm (primary ciliary dyskinesia)
B) Congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens
C) Necrospermia
D) Retrograde ejaculation
Correct Answer: B) Congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens
Explanation:
CFTR gene mutations in cystic fibrosis lead to CBAVD; spermatogenesis is normal, but the resulting obstructive azoospermia causes infertility.
###
Which of the following is the primary inhibitor of LH release from the anterior pituitary?
A) FSH
B) Inhibin B
C) Testosterone
D) Prolactin
Correct Answer: C) Testosterone
Explanation:
Testosterone provides negative feedback on both the hypothalamus (↓GnRH) and pituitary (↓LH). Inhibin B, secreted by Sertoli cells, selectively inhibits FSH rather than LH.
###
Oligospermia is best defined as:
A) Reduced sperm motility
B) Complete absence of sperm in the ejaculate
C) A low sperm concentration below the reference threshold
D) An abnormal percentage of sperm forms on morphology
Correct Answer: C) A low sperm concentration below the reference threshold
Explanation:
Oligospermia: sperm count less than 15 x10⁶/mL , A: Asthenospermia , B: Azoospermia , D: Teratospermia .
###
Teratospermia refers to which of the following semen abnormalities?
A) Absence of sperm in the ejaculate
B) Abnormal sperm motility
C) Abnormal sperm count
D) Abnormal sperm morphology
Correct Answer: D) Abnormal sperm morphology
Explanation:
Teratospermia: sperm morphology below the lower reference limit of normal forms (WHO lower limit ~4% normal forms) , A: Azoospermia , B: Asthenospermia , C: Oligospermia .
###
The majority of seminal fluid volume is contributed by which structure?
A) Seminal vesicles
B) Prostate gland
C) Bulbourethral (Cowper's) glands
D) Epididymis
Correct Answer: A) Seminal vesicles
Explanation:
The seminal vesicles contribute the majority of seminal fluid volume, while the prostate contributes about 1/6, and sperm contribute only about 1%.
###
At which life stages do the major physiological peaks of testosterone production occur in males?
A) Neonatal period and adulthood only
B) Puberty and age 50 only
C) Puberty, age 50, and age 70
D) Embryonic (fetal) development, the neonatal period, and puberty
Correct Answer: D) Embryonic (fetal) development, the neonatal period, and puberty
Explanation:
Testosterone peaks at three distinct life stages: embryonic development, the first few months of neonatal life (mini-puberty), and puberty — driving prostatic and genital development at each stage.
###
A young male bodybuilder using exogenous testosterone is most likely to experience which effect on his fertility?
A) Increased fertility due to higher androgen levels
B) Decreased fertility due to suppression of pituitary FSH and LH
C) No effect on fertility
D) Fertility effects depend only on the route of administration
Correct Answer: B) Decreased fertility due to suppression of pituitary FSH and LH
Explanation:
Exogenous testosterone suppresses hypothalamic-pituitary GnRH/FSH/LH secretion via negative feedback, lowering intratesticular testosterone and impairing spermatogenesis, unlike endogenous testicular testosterone.
###
A patient presents with infertility, small testes, azoospermia, and an elevated FSH level. This clinical picture is most consistent with which of the following?
A) Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (central/secondary cause)
B) Hypergonadotropic hypogonadism (primary testicular failure)
C) Obstructive azoospermia
D) Antisperm antibody-mediated infertility
Correct Answer: B) Hypergonadotropic hypogonadism (primary testicular failure)
Explanation:
Small testes, azoospermia, and elevated FSH indicate primary testicular failure (which is synonymous with hypergonadotropic hypogonadism) ; hypogonadotropic hypogonadism instead presents with low FSH/LH , and obstructive azoospermia typically has a normal FSH.
###
In a patient with azoospermia, which hormone finding suggests an obstructive cause rather than testicular failure?
A) Elevated LH
B) Elevated FSH
C) Normal FSH
D) Decreased FSH
Correct Answer: C) Normal FSH
Explanation:
Obstructive azoospermia typically shows a normal FSH because spermatogenesis is intact, whereas non-obstructive azoospermia (testicular failure) shows an elevated FSH due to loss of Sertoli cell inhibin feedback.
###
The anatomical basis of the blood-testis barrier is formed by which of the following?
A) The basement membrane of the seminiferous tubule
B) Tight junctions between adjacent Sertoli cells
C) Leydig cells within the interstitium
D) Endothelial cells of testicular capillaries
Correct Answer: B) Tight junctions between adjacent Sertoli cells
Explanation:
Sertoli cells are linked by tight junctions that form the blood-testis barrier, creating an immunologically privileged compartment for developing sperm, which form only after puberty.
###
Which of the following is a recognized complication of cryptorchidism?
A) Kidney failure
B) Recurrent UTI only
C) Bladder cancer
D) Infertility
Correct Answer: D) Infertility
Explanation:
Cryptorchidism impairs fertility due to an unfavorable thermal environment for spermatogenesis; it also raises the risk of testicular (not bladder) cancer.
###
What is the karyotype of Klinefelter syndrome?
A) 46,XY
B) 47,XXY
C) 45,XO
D) 46,XX
Correct Answer: B) 47,XXY
Explanation:
Klinefelter syndrome is caused by an extra X chromosome (47,XXY), resulting in small firm testes, gynecomastia, tall stature, and hypergonadotropic hypogonadism with infertility.
###
Infertility is defined as the failure of a couple to achieve pregnancy after which duration of regular, unprotected sexual intercourse?
A) 3 months
B) 6 months
C) 12 months
D) 18 months
E) 24 months
Correct Answer: C) 12 months
Explanation:
Infertility is defined as failure to conceive after 12 months of regular, unprotected intercourse; shorter periods (3–6 months) are not sufficient to establish the diagnosis since most couples conceive within a year.
###
Which of the following pairs correctly represents the prevalence of varicocele in the general male population versus in infertile men?
A) General population 5% — Infertile men 15%
B) General population 15% — Infertile men 35–40%
C) General population 40% — Infertile men 60%
D) General population 15% — Infertile men 20%
E) General population 25% — Infertile men 35%
Correct Answer: B) General population 15% — Infertile men 35–40%
Explanation:
Varicocele affects about 15% of the general male population but is found in roughly 35–40% of infertile men, and is even more common in men with secondary infertility.
###
Which of the following is considered a favorable prognostic factor in male infertility?
A) Long duration of infertility
B) Older female partner age
C) Short duration of infertility
D) Absence of previous pregnancy with the same partner
E) Severe teratospermia
Correct Answer: C) Short duration of infertility
Explanation:
A shorter duration of infertility, younger female partner age, and a history of previous conception are all favorable prognostic factors; the opposite findings (long duration, older maternal age, no prior pregnancy) indicate a poorer prognosis.
###
All of the following are recognized categories of causes of male infertility EXCEPT:
A) Pre-testicular causes (endocrine/hypothalamic-pituitary)
B) Testicular causes (e.g., varicocele, cryptorchidism, Klinefelter syndrome)
C) Post-testicular causes (e.g., obstruction, ejaculatory dysfunction)
D) Idiopathic infertility
E) Purely psychological causes unrelated to any organic pathology
Correct Answer: E) Purely psychological causes unrelated to any organic pathology
Explanation:
Male infertility is classified into pre-testicular, testicular, post-testicular, and idiopathic causes; a purely psychological category unrelated to organic pathology is not part of this recognized classification.
###
All of the following are recognized causes of obstructive azoospermia EXCEPT:
A) Congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens
B) Previous vasectomy
C) Epididymal obstruction following infection
D) Ejaculatory duct obstruction
E) Klinefelter syndrome
Correct Answer: E) Klinefelter syndrome
Explanation:
CBAVD, vasectomy, post-infectious epididymal obstruction, and ejaculatory duct obstruction are classic causes of obstructive azoospermia with preserved spermatogenesis; Klinefelter syndrome causes non-obstructive (testicular failure) azoospermia due to primary testicular failure, not obstruction.
###
Which of the following statements regarding semen fluid analysis in obstructive azoospermia is correct?
A) Typical obstructive azoospermia shows no sperm with usually normal semen volume; ejaculatory duct obstruction specifically shows low volume with acidic, low-fructose semen
B) Typical obstructive azoospermia shows no sperm with low semen volume; ejaculatory duct obstruction shows normal volume with normal fructose
C) Both typical obstructive azoospermia and ejaculatory duct obstruction show low semen volume and low fructose
D) Both typical obstructive azoospermia and ejaculatory duct obstruction show normal semen volume and normal fructose
E) Obstructive azoospermia is always associated with elevated FSH regardless of the level of obstruction
Correct Answer: A) Typical obstructive azoospermia shows no sperm with usually normal semen volume; ejaculatory duct obstruction specifically shows low volume with acidic, low-fructose semen
Explanation:
In general, obstructive azoospermia preserves normal ejaculate volume since spermatogenesis is intact; however, ejaculatory duct obstruction or absent seminal vesicles is an important exception producing low-volume, acidic, low-fructose semen, and FSH remains normal since testicular function is preserved.
###
Which of the following pairs represents andrological conditions correctly associated with varicocele?
A) Male infertility — Testicular hypotrophy/atrophy
B) Retrograde ejaculation — Bladder cancer
C) Priapism — Renal cell carcinoma
D) Klinefelter syndrome — Testicular torsion
E) Epididymo-orchitis — Prostate cancer
Correct Answer: A) Male infertility — Testicular hypotrophy/atrophy
Explanation:
Varicocele is classically associated with male infertility (abnormal semen parameters) and testicular hypotrophy/atrophy due to impaired thermoregulation and venous stasis; the other listed pairings are unrelated conditions not causally linked to varicocele.
